Sources & how this works

Songs, clips and song lengths load from Apple (iTunes) when you use them — previews provided courtesy of iTunes. Starter lists were picked by Encore and checked against Apple's catalog on Nov 4, 2026. The chart list is Apple Music's most-played songs in the US, saved Nov 4, 2026 (chart: Apple). RedWest songs were gathered from setlist.fm, JamBase, Holler, Concerts50 and TourSetlist — per-artist links are on the RedWest page. Concerts were checked by hand on Nov 4, 2026 against Ticketmaster, Live Nation, venue calendars and artist announcements named on each show. This page uses privacy-friendly analytics (Umami) that counts visits anonymously — no cookies, no names, and nothing you type here is collected or stored. Songs and previews load directly from Apple under its own policy. Spot a wrong date or a cancelled show? Tell the person who sent you this link — a wrong show gets taken off the same day, before anything else is checked.
